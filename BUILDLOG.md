# **Day 1 (21\08\26)** 
### *An overall architecture was decided.* 
## *Basic idea:*  
Objects would move through a conveyor system.  
A camera would identify/classify the objects.  
Based on the classification, a mechanism would direct each object into the appropriate bin.  
A microcontroller would control the motors and sorting mechanism.

# **Day 3 (23\08\26)**  
### *Sorting mechanism decided*  
Bins can be placed on a rotating platform and the nuts and bolts will fall into the required bin at the end of the conveyor.

### *Electronic Components were finalised*
Stepper motor (for more control over the movement of conveyor belt)
